# NOTES — variant vB

This line is built on a double verdict: the same five alarms produce two opposite witnesses. The phone, which only sees the alarms being set, concludes its owner is a morning person; the bed, which knows the truth, calls him a liar. The line is 106 characters (one line, well inside the 200-character limit). Against the judge criteria: (1) funny — the punchline turns an inanimate bed into the honest judge of the whole routine, and the contrast between the phone's flattery and the bed's accusation is the laugh; (2) fresh — the joke is not "I snooze my alarm" in general, but the specific, universal ritual of setting a row of alarms as a performance of discipline that fools nobody except the phone; (3) tightly worded — the semicolon splits the line into setup and verdict, "five alarms" is the only setup needed, and "my bed thinks I'm a liar" ends the line on the sharpest possible word, with nothing explained.
